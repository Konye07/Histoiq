What you'll play

Six roles, six ways to know your history

Each role runs its own daily challenge, playable once a day and resumable if you get interrupted. Skip the ones that aren't your thing — every role stands on its own.

A
Annalist
Guess the Year

Pin an event to its year on a sliding timeline — the closer you land, the more it's worth.

H
Herald
Casualty Count

Estimate a battle's true cost. Your score is a multiplier off the real number — lower wins.

O
Officer-of-Arms
Match the Flag Color

Recolor a historical flag's dominant hue by eye until it matches the real thing.

C
Cosmographer
Bigger Territory

Pick the larger of two empires at their historical peak. Survive as long as you can.

C
Chronicler
True or False

Ten historical claims, sudden death — one wrong answer and the run is over.

C
Chancellor
Blind Map Battle

Tap where a battle actually happened on a map with no borders or labels to guide you.
